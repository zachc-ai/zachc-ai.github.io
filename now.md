*Updated November 2026.*

## Building and working

I spend my weekdays working in AI infrastructure and my spare hours tinkering with coding agents and personal workflows. Most of the interesting engineering questions I keep hitting aren't about writing code faster anymore; they're about boundaries, verification, and keeping the loop clean when agents do more of the typing.

I've also been refining this site itself: upgrading my bilingual writing workflow, cleaning up older posts, and experimenting with local voice-cloning narration on Apple Silicon.

## Reading and thinking about

- Coming back to Marcus Aurelius's *Meditations* when I forget the basics (which happens regularly).
- Chewing on Brandon Sanderson's *The Stormlight Archive* and what "journey before destination" looks like in ordinary work.
- Revisiting older essays that aged well, like Kevin Kelly's *1,000 True Fans*, and asking what changes when making things gets nearly free while trust stays expensive.

## Trying to practice

Closing more valves. AI makes it ridiculously easy to spin up ten side threads at once and end the evening drained. Lately I'm trying to do fewer things with cleaner finish lines, protect real daytime focus blocks, and leave part of the weekend completely unscheduled.

## 最近在折腾

工作日做 AI 基础设施，业余时间继续折腾 coding agent 和自己的自动化工作流。越往深里用，越觉得现在卡住人的早不是“写代码够不够快”，而是边界怎么划、验收怎么做，以及 agent 替你敲掉大部分代码之后，自己怎么不失去对系统的掌控。

顺手也在打磨这个网站本身：升级双语润色工作流、把早期的旧文章重新顺一遍，也在 Apple Silicon 上继续试本地克隆声音给文章配朗读。

## 最近在读和在想

- 隔三岔五翻回去读《沉思录》，专治道理都懂、临场就忘的毛病。
- 琢磨布兰登·桑德森《飓光志》里的第一誓言，想想“行先于至”落到日常选择里到底长什么样。
- 重读一些经得住时间的老文章（比如 Kevin Kelly 的《1,000 True Fans》），看做东西越来越便宜之后，哪些东西反倒更值钱。

## 最近在练的一件事

少开阀门。有了 AI 以后，太容易同时起十条支线、到晚上把自己耗干。这阵子在刻意练减法：少开几件事、把开起来的事收好尾，白天护住完整的专注时段，周末留出一段什么都不排的空档。
