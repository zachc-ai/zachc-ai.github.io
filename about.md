I'm Zach.

I like building things with AI, and I like thinking about how people learn, how they make choices, and how to live a little more freely. This is where I write up the tinkering, along with things from books and everyday life that made me stop and think.

A lot of things only make sense from the inside. I want to keep a beginner's curiosity, take the work in front of me seriously, and every so often ask myself whether what keeps me busy is what I actually care about.

Life is an odyssey. Let's compare notes along the way.

## About the site

Most posts are essays. A few are [reading notes]({{ '/digests/' | relative_url }}): the parts of a long article or guide worth keeping.

Every post comes in English and Chinese. The two versions make the same argument, but the Chinese isn't a line-by-line translation; each is written to read naturally on its own. Switch with **EN / 中** at the top of the page, and the site will remember your choice.

Comments are open at the end of each post (they run on GitHub Discussions). To follow along, there's an [RSS feed]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }}).
