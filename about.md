<div class="lang-en" markdown="1">

A place to slow down and process what I'm learning, instead of scrolling past it.

Most posts are essays: on AI and what it changes about building things, on how minds and habits work, on careers and money, and on the odd life event. A few are reading notes, the parts of a long article or guide worth keeping.

Every post comes in English and Chinese. The two versions make the same argument, but the Chinese isn't a line-by-line translation; each is written to read naturally on its own. Switch with **EN / 中** at the top of the page, and the site will remember your choice.

Comments are open at the end of each post (they run on GitHub Discussions). To follow along, there's an [RSS feed](/feed.xml).

</div>

每篇文章都有中英两个版本。观点一样，但中文不是逐句翻译，两边都尽量写得像人话。页面顶部的 **EN / 中** 可以切换，网站会记住你的选择。
