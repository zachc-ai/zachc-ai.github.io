# Preservation and bilingual equivalence

The claims audit checks whether external claims are supported. This check asks a
different question: did editing change what Zach said? Run both; a fluent rewrite
can still fail either one.

## Establish the boundary

- Keep the original draft available and follow the requested scope: preview,
  polish, structural rewrite, or an explicit factual correction.
- Preserve front matter, title, slug, date, filename, language wrappers, quotes,
  code, URLs, source entries, and table data unless the user puts them in scope.
- For a preview, save a separate candidate rather than overwriting the published
  post. A successful check does not authorize committing or publishing.

## Compare before and after

- Do not add or silently drop facts, examples, argument, or substantive caveats.
  Remove redundant wording only when its meaning remains.
- Do not invent a scene, habit, feeling, motive, dialogue, or first-person
  experience to make the prose concrete or more human. Ask for missing material
  only when it blocks the requested work.
- Check who did or said what, numbers and units, negation, comparison groups,
  conditions, chronology, causality, and the strength of the claim. "Sometimes"
  is not "always"; "I think" is not a settled conclusion.
- Preserve useful hesitation, repetition, self-correction, humor, and rough edges
  from the source or voice sample. Do not add them as stylistic decoration.
- Verify protected text against the saved original. Use existing tools where
  useful, but do not install or run an external validator for this workflow.
  Distinguish mechanical checks actually run from semantic review by the model.

## Compare the two languages

Neither language has to come first. Use the author's supplied material and
explicit corrections as the factual boundary, not one language's sentence order.

Check that EN and ZH preserve the same facts, examples, argument, qualifications,
attributions, and ownership of first-person experiences. In particular, do not
turn possibility into certainty, intention into completion, or an observation
into a cause in either version.

Let each language choose its own syntax, idioms, headings, and paragraph breaks.
Natural Chinese and matching facts do not require sentence-by-sentence alignment.
If the drafts already disagree materially, do not silently choose one as true.
Ask about the narrow factual issue or flag it in the preview.

## Repair and stop

Fix only the spans implicated by a preservation or bilingual mismatch, then
recheck the affected content once. Do not restart a whole style pass.

Report PASS only when no material issue remains. Otherwise state the unresolved
issue and do not call the draft ready to publish. Stop once the requested edit
and checks pass; neither a lower word count nor zero style-pattern matches is a
completion target.
