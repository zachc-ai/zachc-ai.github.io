## Hermes Agent

Hermes Agent is a personal, self-hosted assistant that the site owner runs only for himself. It is not offered to the public, and no one else signs in to it.

- **What it accesses:** only the owner's own Google account data (such as calendar events), through the access he grants it.
- **Where it's stored:** on the owner's own NAS, together with its OAuth tokens.
- **How it's processed:** to answer the owner's requests, relevant pieces may be sent to the AI model and speech-to-text APIs the assistant calls. Nothing is sent anywhere else.
- **What it never does:** sell, share, or use the data for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
