When you're young, curiosity hits differently. You stay up late not because anything is due, just because you can't put it down. Everything looks like a puzzle, and pulling on one thread uncovers three more you want to try.

I drifted away from that without noticing. Life got busy, and I got pretty good at executing, staying efficient, and knowing what to ignore. None of those are bad traits. Efficiency and curiosity just pull in opposite directions: one narrows, the other wanders. For about a decade, I mostly picked narrowing.

Then AI showed up. Not the hype-cycle version, the moment you actually sit down with a tool and realize what one person can build just changed. I started with small experiments. Before long I was staying up late again, waking up with ideas, and tinkering on weekends for no deadline and no audience, just to see what would happen.

It took me a while to place the feeling. It was the same rush I had as a kid learning to code, and the same mindset I fell back on every time life dropped me into a new place as an underdog with no reputation and no playbook. Just curiosity and a willingness to figure it out.

What changed, I think, is that AI flattened the starting line enough to make me feel like a beginner again. When tools are this new, nobody has ten years of experience. The people moving fastest are simply the ones willing to poke at things and break them. I know that underdog seat well. Every time I've been in it, I found my way through by staying in the room longer than I felt comfortable, not by being the smartest person there.

I'd forgotten how alive that feels. If you've been running on autopilot for a while, optimizing everything and wondering about nothing, find something that makes you feel clumsy again. That discomfort of not knowing is where my curiosity came back.

I let it slip away once. I'd rather hang on to it this time.

<div class="lang-zh lang-hidden" markdown="1">

年轻时候的好奇心不太一样。熬夜不为交差，纯粹是停不下来。看什么都像谜题，刚弄明白一样，顺手又勾出三个想试的。

不知道从哪天起，这股劲儿悄悄淡了。日子越来越忙，我也越来越擅长执行、抓重点、把事干利索。这些本事当然都挺好，只是效率和好奇心往两个相反的方向拉：一个往里收，一个往外散。差不多有十年，我一直在往里收。

直到 AI 来了。不是新闻里那个被说烂的概念，而是真坐下来上手之后，突然发现一个人能折腾出来的东西变了。一开始只是顺手做点小实验，没多久我又开始熬夜，早上睁眼脑子里全是念头，周末自己鼓捣东西，不赶 deadline 也不给谁看，就是想看看跑起来会怎样。

过了好一阵我才认出这种感觉。跟小时候学写代码时一模一样，也跟我每一次被扔进陌生环境、从头起步时一样：没名气，没现成套路，手里只有好奇心和一点硬着头皮摸索的劲儿。

大概是 AI 把起跑线拉平了不少，我才终于觉得自己可以再当一回新手。工具这么新，谁都没有十年经验，跑得快的无非是肯动手试、不怕搞砸的人。这种从低处往上爬的位置我熟。以前每次落到这儿，最后能走出路来，靠的也不是我最聪明，就是肯留在场上多折腾一会儿。

我都快忘了那种整个人活过来的感觉。如果你也有一阵子像开了自动驾驶，整天忙着把已知的事做快、很少再对什么发问，不妨找件让自己重新当回菜鸟的事。我的好奇心，就是在那种答不上来的别扭里找回来的。

这东西我丢过一次，这回想尽量留住。
