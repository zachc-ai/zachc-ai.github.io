In high school, the principal gave a short talk every Monday after the flag-raising. One week, his topic was *shendu* (慎独).

I didn't really get it. And I didn't do it either. When there were no adults home, I'd turn on the TV and leave the homework for later.

The word stuck, though.

## What it actually says

It comes from the *Doctrine of the Mean*: "莫见乎隐，莫显乎微，故君子慎其独也。" Roughly: nothing shows more than what you hide, nothing gives you away like the small stuff, so a decent person is most careful when alone.

Simple enough idea. Doing it is the hard part.

## Why I didn't do it

Looking back, the standard back then belonged to other people. A teacher checked the homework. Someone proctored the exam. Someone handed out the grades.

So when somebody was watching, I did the work, and when nobody was, the TV went on. I think what I really cared about was whether I'd get caught.

## Now

It's different now. I'm the one I have to answer to.

I've set a bar for myself, and I push hard to clear it. Nobody has to tell me to. That bar is already higher than anything anyone else asks of me, so I can just focus on my own.

Sometimes, late at night with nobody around, I'll keep working until 3 or 4 a.m., because there's a goal I set for myself and I want to get it done.

Same situation, nobody watching. As a kid, I turned on the TV. Now I keep working.

And once the bar is mine, whether anyone's watching starts to matter a lot less.

It took me years to get what my principal was talking about that Monday. Some of it, anyway.

That's it for now.

高中的时候，每周一升完旗，校长有个国旗下讲话。有一回他讲的是“慎独”。

那时候我没太听懂。说实话，也没做好。家里没大人的时候，我会开电视，作业先放一边。

不过这个词，我倒是记住了。

## 它到底在说什么

慎独出自《礼记·中庸》，原话是：“莫见乎隐，莫显乎微，故君子慎其独也。”

用大白话说，差不多是：越是藏着的东西，越容易露出来；越是小事，越能看出一个人。所以一个人待着的时候，反而要更小心。

道理不复杂嘛，难的是做到。

## 那时候为什么没做好

回头看，那时候的标准是别人的。作业有老师查，考试有人监考，做得怎么样有人打分。

有人看着，我就好好做；没人看着，电视就开了。我那会儿在意的，其实是会不会被查到吧。

## 现在呢

现在不一样了，我是真的要对自己负责。

我给自己定了一个 bar，要求自己一定做到。这股劲儿是我自己的，不用谁催。这个 bar 已经比别人对我的任何要求都高了，所以我可以只管自己的要求。

有时候夜深人静，没人看着，我会自己加班到三四点，就为了把给自己定的目标做完。

同样是没人看着。小时候我开电视，现在我接着干活。

标准是自己的了，有没有人看着，好像就没那么要紧了。

校长那天讲的，我是隔了这么多年才懂了一点。

先写到这儿。
